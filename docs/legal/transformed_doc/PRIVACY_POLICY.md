**5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS (GDPR / CCPA / OCPA / CPA)**

Depending on your geographic location or state of residence (including California, Colorado, and Oregon), you possess specific statutory privacy rights regarding your personal information under frameworks like the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act (OCPA). **The Right to Deletion:** The right to request that we permanently erase your personal data files from our cloud servers and active databases.

**The Right to Correction:** The right to request modifications to inaccurate or outdated personal account information.

**The Right to Non-Discrimination:** The Company strictly guarantees we will never deny service, alter pricing, or degrade performance if you choose to exercise your privacy choices.
