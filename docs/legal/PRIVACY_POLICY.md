# Privacy Policy

**Fraternal Council Archives™** · Effective **October 26, 2026**

## What the platform keeps

Fraternal Council Archives™ keeps the information your council needs to run: your name, contact details and member number; your offices and membership history; the shifts you sign up for and the hours you log; your expense reports and receipts; donations, charity requests, meeting minutes, and the photos, diary entries, prayer intentions and recordings you choose to share.

## Secret ballots

When you vote by secret ballot, the platform records that you voted, so you cannot vote twice, but the stored ballot never names you. Officers see only the totals.

## Who can see it

Fellow members of your council can see the roster and your contact details. Officers see the records their office needs, such as expense reports at the desks that sign them. Platform administrators can open records only to support your council.

## How it is used

Your council uses this information only for council business. It is not sold, and it is not shared for advertising.

When your council connects its own Google Drive, network file server or email account, the photos, documents and messages sent there are kept under that account and its own terms.

## Your choices

To correct your information, open My Profile. To ask what the platform holds about you, or to ask for a record to be removed, speak to your Grand Knight or Financial Secretary.
